# United States Requirements

Federal mandates for software supply chain security and procurement attestations.

## Concepts

- [CISA 2026 Minimum Elements](cisa-minimum-elements-2026.md): Updated baseline SBOM requirements (July 2026).
- [NTIA Minimum Elements](ntia-minimum-elements.md): Initial 2021 federal baseline (Superseded).
- [CISA Secure Software Self-Attestation](cisa-self-attestation.md): Common attestation form for NIST SSDF.
- [OMB Memoranda M-22-18 and M-23-16](omb-memoranda-m-22-18-m-23-16.md): White House procurement directives.
- [NIST SP 800-218 (SSDF Requirements)](ssdf-nist-sp-800-218.md): Secure development practice requirements.
