# Summary

**prEN 40000-1-3** is the draft European standard developed by CEN/CENELEC Joint Technical Committee 13 (JTC 13) pursuant to the European Commission's standardization request under the Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)[^cencenelec-standardization].

**Binding / contractual / guidance**: Voluntary harmonised technical standard; citation in the OJEU confers legal presumption of conformity with CRA Annex I Part II(1).

# Scope and Technical Focus
- **Harmonized SBOM Profile**: Specifies minimal data structures conforming to CRA Annex I Part II(1) requirements, leveraging established international standards (SPDX 2.3 / 3.0, CycloneDX 1.7).
- **Machine Readability and Packaging**: Establishes distribution protocols, digital signature standards, and delivery formats for technical files.
