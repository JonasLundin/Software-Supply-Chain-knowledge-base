# Definition

Under Article 3(1) of the Cyber Resilience Act, a **product with digital elements** means any software or hardware product and its remote data processing solutions, including software or hardware components placed on the market separately[^eu-cra-regulation].

# Context
PDEs represent the primary regulatory target of the Cyber Resilience Act, triggering mandatory SBOM generation, essential vulnerability handling duties, and CE marking.
