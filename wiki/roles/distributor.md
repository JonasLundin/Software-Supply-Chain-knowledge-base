# Summary

Under the Cyber Resilience Act (Article 19), a **distributor** is any natural or legal person in the supply chain, other than the manufacturer or importer, who makes a product with digital elements available on the Union market[^eu-cra-regulation].

# Obligations in the Supply Chain
- **Due Care Verification**: Confirming that the product bears the CE marking, includes required user instructions, and that the manufacturer has satisfied basic regulatory requirements.
- **Traceability Maintenance**: Retaining transactional records identifying upstream suppliers and downstream recipients to facilitate rapid security recalls.
