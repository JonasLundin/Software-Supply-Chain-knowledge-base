# EU law

CRA and NIS2 supply-chain provisions (pointers to the CRA and NIS2 bundles).

## Concepts

- [NIS2 Directive Article 21(2)(d): Supply Chain Security](nis2-article-21-supply-chain.md) — Statutory requirements for essential and important entities to assess and mitigate cybersecurity risks across their ICT supply chain.
- [Cyber Resilience Act: Supply Chain Provisions (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)](cra-supply-chain.md) — Statutory supply-chain cybersecurity obligations for manufacturers, open-source stewards, and economic operators under the CRA.
