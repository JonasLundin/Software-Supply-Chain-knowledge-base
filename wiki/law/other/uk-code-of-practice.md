# Summary

The UK Government Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (**DSIT**) issued the **Code of Practice for Software Vendors**, setting voluntary baselines for software supply chain security[^regulation-eu-2024-2847].

# Principles
- Secure software development lifecycle and continuous build security.
- Comprehensive vulnerability disclosure policy (VDP) and coordination.
- Maintaining component transparency and dependency governance.
