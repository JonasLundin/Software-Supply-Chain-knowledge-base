# Summary

Signed by the President on May 12, 2021, **Executive Order 14028** directed comprehensive enhancements to United States federal government cybersecurity[^us-eo-14028].

> [!NOTE]
> **Amendments**: EO 14028 was subsequently amended by **Executive Order 14144** in January 2025 and **Executive Order 14306** on June 6, 2025, expanding federal cyber supply chain and vulnerability management mandates.

# Key Supply-Chain Directives (Section 4)
- **NIST Guidance**: Directed NIST to issue guidelines for secure software development environments (resulting in NIST SP 800-218 SSDF).
- **Minimum Elements for SBOM**: Directed NTIA (and subsequently CISA) to publish minimum elements for an SBOM.
- **Federal Procurement Attestation**: Mandated federal agencies to require software suppliers to attest to compliance with NIST secure development practices.
