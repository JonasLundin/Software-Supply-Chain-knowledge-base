# Summary

**Executive Order 14028** (May 2021) directed the modernization of federal cybersecurity, introducing federal requirements for Software Bills of Materials (SBOMs) and NIST SP 800-218 conformity[^regulation-eu-2024-2847].

# Federal Procurement Directives
- Section 4 mandates guidelines outlining minimum elements for an SBOM.
- Requires federal software vendors to attest to secure software development practices.
