# Software Supply Chain Knowledge Base

Welcome to the **Software Supply Chain Knowledge Base**, a structured, open knowledge repository covering software supply chain integrity, SBOM specifications, attestation and provenance frameworks, vulnerability disclosure and VEX, package identifiers, and regulatory baselines.

## Knowledge Base Sections

- [Standards](standards/index.md): Technical specifications for SBOM formats, attestations, VEX, frameworks, and identifiers.
- [Requirements](requirements/index.md): Concrete regulatory and procurement requirements across the EU, US, and Germany.
- [Procedures](procedures/index.md): Operational engineering practices for build integration, signing, verification, and due diligence.
- [Roles](roles/index.md): Economic and technical roles across software supply chains.
- [Law](law/index.md): Primary statutory instruments (CRA, NIS2, US Executive Orders).
- [Guidance](guidance/index.md): Government and community implementation guidance.
- [Jurisdictions](jurisdictions/index.md): Country and regional supply chain policy profiles.
- [Timeline](timeline/index.md): Chronology of major milestones, specification releases, and enforcement dates.
- [Glossary](glossary/index.md): Formal legal and technical definitions.
