# Summary

**NIST Special Publication 800-161 Revision 1** provides systematic guidance on managing cybersecurity risks throughout supply chains for information and communications technology (ICT) products and services[^nist-sp-800-161r1].

# Multi-Tier Risk Governance
- **Tier 1 (Organization)**: Governance policies, executive leadership oversight, and supply chain risk tolerance definitions.
- **Tier 2 (Mission / Business Process)**: Supplier evaluation criteria, contractual security controls, and key risk indicators.
- **Tier 3 (Operational / System)**: Component verification, SBOM ingestion, automated vulnerability analysis, and supplier risk assessments.
