# Attestation and Provenance Standards

Cryptographic envelopes, transparency systems, and provenance frameworks.

## Concepts

- [SLSA v1.2](slsa-1-2.md): Supply chain Levels for Software Artifacts v1.2.
- [SLSA v1.0](slsa-1-0.md): Foundational provenance levels (Superseded).
- [in-toto](in-toto.md): CNCF Graduated supply chain attestation framework.
- [Sigstore](sigstore.md): Keyless cryptographic signing infrastructure (Cosign, Fulcio, Rekor).
- [DSSE](dsse.md): Dead Simple Signing Envelope format.
- [SCITT](scitt.md): Supply Chain Integrity, Transparency, and Trust (IETF).
