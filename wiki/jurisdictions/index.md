# Jurisdictions

One page per jurisdiction with SBOM, attestation or provenance expectations, naming the instrument, the authority and the date.

## Concepts

- [Jurisdiction: European Union](european-union.md) — European regulatory regime mandating horizontal SBOM inventories, vulnerability handling, and supply chain security under the CRA and NIS2.
- [Jurisdiction: Germany (BSI)](germany.md) — German Federal Office for Information Security (BSI) technical standards and procurement guidelines for cyber resilience and SBOMs.
- [Jurisdiction: Japan (METI)](japan.md) — Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) cybersecurity guidelines for software supply chain management in critical industries.
- [Jurisdiction: United Kingdom](united-kingdom.md) — UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) and NCSC software supply chain governance and Code of Practice.
- [Jurisdiction: United States](united-states.md) — United States federal framework driving SBOM adoption, secure software development attestation, and federal procurement standards.
