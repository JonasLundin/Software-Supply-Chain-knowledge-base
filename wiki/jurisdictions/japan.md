# Summary

**Japan**, guided by the **Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI)** and the Information-technology Promotion Agency (IPA), has implemented aggressive national strategies to secure software supply chains across automotive, electronics, and industrial manufacturing sectors[^ntia-sbom-elements].

# METI Guidelines & Initiatives

- **Cybersecurity Guidelines for Software Supply Chain Management**: Framework providing concrete steps for manufacturers to audit suppliers, mandate component tracking, and manage zero-day risks.
- **Automotive & Medical Device Focus**: Strict enforcement of SBOM integration in connected vehicles (UN R155 compliance) and medical device software safety.
- **International Harmonization**: METI actively participates in international standards harmonization, endorsing SPDX (ISO/IEC 5962) and CycloneDX.
