# Summary

In **Japan**, the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and the Information-technology Promotion Agency (IPA) promote software supply chain security through national SBOM guidelines[^meti-japan-sbom-guide].

# Key Initiatives
- **METI SBOM Guidelines**: Published in July 2023, providing implementation roadmaps for Japanese software vendors and industrial manufacturers.
- **Proof-of-Concept Pilot Projects**: Cross-industry pilot implementations in automotive, medical devices, and critical infrastructure sectors.
