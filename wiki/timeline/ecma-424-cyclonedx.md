# Milestone

In **June 2024**, Ecma International approved **ECMA-424 1st Edition** based on CycloneDX 1.6, followed by **ECMA-424 2nd Edition** in **December 2025** based on CycloneDX 1.7[^cyclonedx-1-7-spec].

# Significance
- Provided formal de jure recognition to the OWASP CycloneDX specification, aiding public procurement in European and international jurisdictions.

**June 2024**: Ecma International General Assembly formally approves **ECMA-424**, officially standardizing the **CycloneDX** Software Bill of Materials format as an international standard[^cyclonedx-1-7-spec].

# International Standardization Context

Standardization through Ecma International provided CycloneDX with formal recognition by a recognized international standards development organization (SDO). ECMA-424 establishes a rigorous normative baseline for representing software components, hardware components, services, cryptographic assets, and vulnerability exploitability information. The 2nd Edition (approved in 2025) integrated CycloneDX v1.7, introducing extended support for attestation lifecycles, machine learning bills of materials (ML-BOM), and regulatory reporting profiles.
