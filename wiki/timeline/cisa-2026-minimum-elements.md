# Milestone

On **July 29, 2026**, the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) published the final **2026 Minimum Elements for an SBOM**, officially updating and replacing the 2021 NTIA minimum elements[^cisa-minimum-elements-2026].

# Significance
- Updated federal procurement standards to require component licensing, generation context, tool versions, and cryptographic hashes.

**2026**: Anticipated publication and federal adoption of the **CISA 2026 Minimum Elements for Software Bills of Materials (SBOM)**[^cisa-minimum-elements-2026], updating the 2021 NTIA baseline.

# Expanded Scope and Operational Evolution

Drawing upon five years of operational SBOM implementation across federal agencies and critical infrastructure, CISA's 2026 update establishes advanced requirements beyond basic component inventories. Key evolutions include mandatory inclusion of cryptographic component hashes, standardized Package URLs (purl), explicit tracking of nested and transitive dependencies, automated verification of toolchain provenance, and direct integration with machine-readable VEX advisories to separate exploitable vulnerabilities from unimpacted dependencies.
