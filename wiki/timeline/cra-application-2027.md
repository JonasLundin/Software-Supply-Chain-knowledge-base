# Summary

**11 December 2027**: General date of application for Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act)[^eu-cra-regulation], thirty-six months following its entry into force.

# Legal Effect for Supply Chain Integrity

From this date, all products with digital elements placed on the European Union market must comply with the essential cybersecurity requirements set out in Annex I Part I and the vulnerability handling requirements in Annex I Part II. Manufacturers must maintain an internal Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) identifying all third-party components, conduct dependency due diligence, establish automated patch distribution mechanisms, and fulfill regulatory reporting obligations for actively exploited vulnerabilities within statutory deadlines.
